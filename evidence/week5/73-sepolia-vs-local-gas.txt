Reading. MockNZD takes no constructor argument and costs the same on both
networks to the unit. Every other difference is a multiple of 12 gas, the
price gap between a non-zero and a zero calldata byte (16 against 4): the
constructor arguments are addresses, and the two deployments' addresses have
different bytes. The code executed charged the same gas on both networks: they
price contract creation, constructor storage writes and calldata the same,
which is consistent with one gas schedule. It does not measure the opcodes a
later call adds, such as the token transfer in payLevy; for those, ADR-017
states the same schedule as an assumption.
